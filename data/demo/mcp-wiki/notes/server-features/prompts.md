# Prompts

Prompts are templated messages and workflows for users, listed with `prompts/list` and fetched with `prompts/get`. They are user-controlled, unlike model-controlled [[tools]]. Lists are cacheable ([[pagination-and-caching]]). Related: [[resources]], [[skills-over-mcp]].

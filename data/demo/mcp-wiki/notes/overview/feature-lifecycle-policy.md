# Feature lifecycle and deprecation policy

Adopted with 2026-07-28 (SEP-2596): features are Active, Deprecated or Removed, with a minimum twelve-month deprecation window and a registry of deprecated features. See [[deprecated-features]] and [[spec-2026-07-28]].

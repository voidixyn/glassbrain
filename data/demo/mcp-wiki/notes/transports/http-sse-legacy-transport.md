# HTTP+SSE transport (legacy)

The 2024-11-05 transport, replaced by [[streamable-http-transport]] in 2025-03-26 and now classified Deprecated. Clients detect it by a failed POST followed by a GET returning an `endpoint` event. See [[deprecated-features]].
